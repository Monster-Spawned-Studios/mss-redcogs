# The Monster Spawned Studios Code for Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, level of experience, education, and socioeconomic status.

As such, it is expected that you treat everyone with respect and dignity, and that you do not engage in any form of harassment, discrimination, or any other form of inappropriate behavior. Remember, we all started out as beginners at some point, and we all have something to learn from each other. No one is perfect, and we all make mistakes. We are all human, we all have the same, fundamental, rights and at the end of the day, we all bleed the same color.

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

### Examples of unacceptable behavior include

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances (whether in text form, verbally, or in any other form).
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address(es), names, or any other form of PII (personal identification information), without explicit permission.
- Threats that disparage or defame.
- Excessive language, such as swearing, derogatory comments, or any other form of language that could be considered offensive or inappropriate in a professional setting.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Please keep in mind, that any and all violations of the code of conduct will be subject to interpretation by the Monster Spawned Studios team. We are more than happy to discuss your concerns with you or other contributors. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us at [https://monsterspawned.com/contact/](https://monsterspawned.com/contact/).

## Our Responsibilities

The Monster Spawned Studios team is responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

### Giving and receiving feedback

We want our community to be a safe place for all, regardless of gender, race, ethnicity, religion, or political affiliation. We expect all contributors to respect each other and give constructive feedback. If you have an issue with a potential violation of the code of conduct, please let us know as soon as possible so that we can fix it.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within community spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the community or its members. Examples of representing a community include using the Monster Spawned Studios Discord server, the Monster Spawned Studios website, or any other online or offline community space that is associated or affiliated with the Monster Spawned Studios team (such as the various game servers that are hosted by the Monster Spawned Studios team, the official Steam community, etc.).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the Monster Spawned Studios team at [https://monsterspawned.com/contact/](https://monsterspawned.com/contact/) and all related parties will be notified and addressed. The Monster Spawned Studios team will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.
